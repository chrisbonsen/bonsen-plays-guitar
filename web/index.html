Pitch Game

Hear a note, sing it back, and hold it in the zone for one second. Any octave counts, so it works for every voice. Find a quiet room and turn your volume up. Your voice is analysed on this device only. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.

How to get better at it

  • Hum first, then open up to "ah". Humming is easier to keep in tune.
  • Slide into it. Start a little low and glide up until the needle centres. Sliding teaches your voice where notes live.
  • Mostly flat? Think "up", lift your eyebrows and use more breath. Mostly sharp? Relax and stop pushing.
  • Can't hear the note on your phone speaker? Pluck it on your guitar: the A, D and G strings are those notes. B is the second-thinnest string.
  • Little and often beats long sessions. Three rounds a day earns XP, and that's plenty.

How XP works

  • 1 XP per minute played (max 120 a day), +20 for a full-length show
  • Streak bonus: +2 XP per night of your streak (max +30) every day you play
  • +30 per lesson passed, +120 bonus for a headline show (boss song)
  • +50 per skill, +75 per song crossed off the setlist, +25 per dare, +10 per speed record
  • +15 per pitch game round (up to 3 a day)
  • Badges give bonus XP
